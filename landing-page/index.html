FAQ

Common questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes! The Starter plan is completely free forever. Pro comes with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored in ISO 27001-certified data centres. We offer EU and US region selection, and Enterprise customers can bring their own storage.

Does NovaMind support team collaboration?

Yes! Pro and Enterprise plans include shared workspaces, role-based access, and real-time collaboration features.
